Frailty screening · Abu Dhabi

Catching frailty before it becomes a fall.

FrailTriage turns the 2-minute Timed Up & Go walk test into an early-warning system. One sensor on the lower back picks up the movement changes a stopwatch misses.

The problem

The UAE is ageing fast, and falls are already the leading injury.

29%

of the UAE population will be 65 or older by 2050, up from 6% in 2017.

Dubai Health Authority
~60%

of hospitalized injuries in older UAE patients are caused by falls.

Hefny et al., Afr Health Sci, 2016
33%

of older Emiratis in Abu Dhabi are already pre-frail, and pass today's walk test as “fine”.

Al-Kuwaiti et al., Front. Public Health, 2015

The gap: the Timed Up & Go only flags frailty once it slows a person's walk. By then it is often too late to act early.

The solution

Same test. One sensor. A much earlier signal.

Nothing changes for the patient or the clinic routine. The test they already do becomes the screening.

  1. Wear

    One inertial sensor on the sacrum, at the lower back.

  2. Walk

    The same 2-minute Timed Up & Go test clinics run today.

  3. Analyze

    The model extracts 50+ movement features hidden from a stopwatch: phase timing, sway, turn smoothness.

  4. Flag

    A risk score catches frailty even when gait speed looks normal.

The opportunity

A hidden risk pool no current tool can see.

About 1 in 8 patients who pass today's walk test as “normal” already move like a frail patient. They leave the clinic with a clean result and no follow-up.

8 people pass the walk test · 1 moves like a frail patient

Evidence

Validated without leakage, one person held out at a time.

All results use leave-one-subject-out cross-validation (LOSO). Confidence intervals are bootstrapped at participant level with 3,000 resamples.

Two phases carry the most weight

Walk-back and walk-out duration, both measured at the sacrum, are the two strongest predictors.

Wrist adds little

Wrist alone reaches 0.732 AUC, and adding it to the sacrum does not improve on the sacrum alone.

Robust to how features are chosen

Lasso, mutual information and Random Forest selection from all 54 features all land between 0.82 and 0.90 AUC.
